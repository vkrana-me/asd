# Keep learning data local and require consent for remote AI

Repository-Based Courses, Course Snapshots, progress, Personal Notes, and generated material remain on the Learner's device with no account, synchronization, or telemetry. The Learner configures an OpenAI-compatible provider, and the product sends only the minimum selected Repository Evidence after per-course consent for course generation, lesson regeneration, or Tutor requests; this preserves the local-first experience while enabling AI-guided learning.
